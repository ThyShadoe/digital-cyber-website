Digital Literacy
Technical Skills
  • Navigating devices (computers, tablets, mobile phones): Learn how to use different gadgets like computers and phones to get around the digital world.
  • Using applications and productivity software: Figure out how to use programs like Word or Google Docs to get things done.
Information and Research Skills
  • Searching and filtering information: Find what you need online by using search engines and picking out the right stuff.
  • Identifying credible and unreliable sources: Know which websites and sources you can trust for real information.
  • Understanding digital footprints: Be aware of what you leave behind when you go online.
Digital Communication
  • Email basics and conventions: Learn how to write and send emails the right way.
  • Online interaction and responsible communication: Be nice and careful when you talk to people online.
  • Collaborative tools and platforms: Use websites and apps to work together with others.
Creating Digital Content
  • Document and Media Creation: Make your own stuff like reports, videos, and pictures.
  • Ethical use of materials (Copyright and Intellectual property): Use other people's work the right way and don't steal it.
  • Awareness of audience and online impact: Think about who will see your stuff and how it might affect them.
