Cyber Safety
Protect your Personal Information
  • What Data is considered Personal : Personal data includes your name, address, phone number, or anything that identifies you.
  • How Websites and Apps collect Information: Websites and apps gather details about you when you use them, like your location or browsing habits.
  • Practical ways to limit data exposure: Use privacy settings and avoid sharing unnecessary personal details online.
Password and Account Security
  • Characteristics of strong passwords : Good passwords are long, unique, and include letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Two-factor authentication: Adding a second step, like a code sent to your phone, helps protect your account even if someone has your password.
  • Regular account review and updates: Check your account settings often and update passwords regularly for better security.
Recognizing Online Threats
  • Phishing attempts: Be cautious of emails or messages that try to trick you into giving personal info.
  • Fake websites and online fraud: Watch out for websites that look real but are designed to steal your money or info.
  • Malware and suspicious downloads : Avoid downloading files or apps from untrusted sources that could harm your device.
Safe Use of Social Media
  • Privacy settings and audience control: Control who can see your posts and personal info on social media.
  • Risks of oversharing: Sharing too much about yourself can put you at risk of harm or theft.
  • Handling negative or unsafe interactions : Block, report, or ignore people who upset or threaten you online.
Cyberbullying Awareness
  • Forms of online harassment: Cyberbullying can include mean messages, spreading rumors, or posting hurtful comments.
  • How to document incidents: Save screenshots or messages if someone is bullying you online.
  • Reporting systems on digital platforms: Use the platform’s tools to report cyberbullying or harmful behavior.
